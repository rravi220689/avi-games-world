Pilot FAQ

How to earn Cosmic Credits faster?
Race on Hard Mode to earn a 1.6x (60%) multiplier on all podium rewards, or collect gold in Super Cosmic Gold Rush!
How do track hurdles work?
Pulsing electric laser gates appear across circuits. Hitting them discharges deflector shields, slows your craft, and triggers phone vibration. Steer clear or boost through!
Can I play on mobile devices?
Yes! All 3D tracks and Arcade games feature responsive on-screen touch pedals (Left, Right, Nitro, Brake, Boost) and fluid touch dragging.
Where do I upgrade my spacecraft?
Head to The Hangar (`/hangar/`) to upgrade Top Speed, Acceleration, Handling Grip, Deflector Shields, and Nitro up to Mk V, plus customize your paint livery!
